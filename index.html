Lesson 1: Understanding Cells & Ranges

What is a cell?

A cell is the intersection of a row and a column. Every cell has an address such as A1, B2 or C10.

What is a range?

A range is a group of cells. For example, A2:C10 includes columns A through C and rows 2 through 10.

Try it

Open Google Sheets and create three columns: Product, Sales, and Profit. Enter three products and their values.

Practice goal: Identify the cell address of each value and select the range containing your complete table.
